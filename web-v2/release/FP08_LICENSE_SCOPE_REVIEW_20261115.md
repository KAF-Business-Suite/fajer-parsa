- Visa and residence: **preliminary enquiry only**, not a visa authority, approval or issuance promise. Other countries' entry rules require source-specific review.
- Business invitations: **preliminary enquiry only**; no claim of independent immigration/consular authority.
- Flights and hotels: **coordination enquiry only** until authorized supplier contracts, pricing/refund terms and channels are verified.
- Pilgrimage groups: **preliminary enquiry only**; relevant service endorsement, suppliers and program conditions unverified.
- Documents and consular: **information/enquiry only**; issuance belongs to authorized authorities.
- Travel support: **coordination enquiry only** until service scope, staff capacity, supplier and emergency arrangements are validated.
The 15 destinations are categories of potential enquiry, **not fifteen approved visa products**.
